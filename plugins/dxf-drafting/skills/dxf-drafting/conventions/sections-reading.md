## 1. Think like the structural engineer - never read a beam from its label alone
Before choosing a recipe read the PLAN around the cut strip (`scripts/plan_cuts.py`, `scripts/cut_crossings.py FILE --box ...`, `star_plan.py` listing):
1. **What the cut line crosses decides the section**, classified by LAYER: `93_KORA` beam faces (the two crossings B apart; the CLOSING segment of a closed outline counts) -> `8_KIR` existing wall (two parallel faces 8..60 apart) -> `9_KONTUR` free slab edge -> nothing (skip + report). A beam face lying ON a `9_KONTUR` line (<= 3) = the beam IS the slab edge: no slab on that side (`general_beam.py --sides left|right` = the side that HAS a slab, the other face runs full height); a `9_KONTUR` farther out = cantilever slab (the side exists). **A beam on the edge of the building** (no structural geometry - `93_KORA` / `9_KONTUR` / walls / column hatches, `star_plan.OUTER_LAYERS` - beyond one face within 1000 along the beam) likewise has NO slab on that side (`outer_sides`, user 2026-10-25, test_sections 1-1 / 3-3 east beams: 'no floor plan and no ceiling there'; 1 file, UNVERIFIED elsewhere); the planner note says 'no floor beyond it'. A WALL with ceilings on both sides is NOT a free edge: general untagged-beam section with B = wall thickness (V, 2-2 of brenda_karka).
2. **B and H come from the cut's OWN strip**: B between the two `93_KORA` faces along the cut (the user's mark pair is only roughly centred); H from the `b/h` tag that sits on the strip or the segment label `H=nnn` (o.k./u.k.) that starts at the cut. A tag such as `100/270` beside the strip usually belongs to the CROSSING beam - never use it (first 17-17 drew 100x270, real 80x80). `b/h`: h includes the slab. Slanted beams: size from the `b/h o.k...` label near the cut (slabs assumed, ?).
3. **Untagged beam** (no `b/h`): B from the plan geometry, height = the PARAMETER `h` drawn at the minimum 40 (? 40 is a guess; the height dims carry the symbol `h` / `h-6`), widths and slab D numeric. Gallery 5-5 (B=25) accepted. Untagged with a wall/pier rising from it: stirrups transfer-style, symbol `h`.
4. **Ledge ('tooth') read from the dashed line** (user 2026-10-15, 6-6): outer face, `15_DASHED` line (= face of the WALL ABOVE, hidden from below), other outer face. Wall part = the STEM, the rest (15) = the LEDGE carrying the panels. One `0_LUHADIM_SHEN` line 15 inside a face = ledge on that side. The hollow-core bay (`D=20+5`, mesh note, span arrows, LENGTHWISE) = ledge side; the other side = solid slab / room / plain face. Compare the LEVELS of both sides (`-4.40=+66.10` vs `-4.55=+65.95` = 15 apart). Walls above (`82_KIR_OLE`) make a TRANSFER candidate; an opening in the wall at the cut means no wall in the section itself.
5. **Panel span vs cut (user's explanation of 1-1, V)**: the plan draws double arrows (LEADERs on `0_LUHADIM_HEZ`) beside the `D=a+b` label = panel span. View PARALLEL to the span (cut crosses the panels): cores show as CIRCLES / obrounds, panels run past and do NOT bear -> **cast edge beam** with the hollow-core edge next to it (`ledge_beam.py --kind cast`). View PERPENDICULAR to the span: panels seen LENGTHWISE and BEAR on the ledges -> **ledge beam** (`--kind ledge`). `plan_cuts.py` prints CORES / LENGTHWISE per cut; never assume solid slabs.
5b. **Same beam, two cuts**: the pairs of one group may differ (a wall above one of them): print and report every distinct note of the group; a wall above a beam / a roller shutter on a beam with slab both sides is drawn with `beam_shutter.py` (sections-drawing.md), on a free edge with `slab_edge --shutter`; `star_plan` only detects it.
6. **A tagged beam is the general SOLID beam unless the plan shows hollow-core evidence** (a `D=a+b` label or LENGTHWISE panels): a plan without hollow-core slabs never gets `ledge_beam --slab 20 --topping 5`.

## 2. Slab beside the beam, per side
- **Hollow or solid**: `D=a+b` (ground floor 25+7, mezzanine/gallery 20+5) = hollow-core panel a + topping b; `D=nn` WITHOUT `+` = SOLID cast slab (3-3, 4-4, 14-14, 17-17). A raised (double) floor label `D=10` at a higher level above a `D=30` slab: not structural, NEVER drawn (user 2026-10-15).
- **Thickness and level come from the plan, per side** (never a D=25 template): the slab block `S_M` (attribs `D=nn` and a level; a block may hold TWO `D=` attribs - the last is the visible one) and `D=` texts - search BOTH. Per side of the viewer (view N: west is left, S: east, E: north, W: south): the marker whose straight line from the beam face crosses the fewest `93_KORA` / `9_KONTUR` / wall segments (penalty 1500 each; 3+ walls reject it), 25-700 along the cut (widen to 900 when a side is empty), score hypot(along, 2 x beamwise), perp < 350. A marker BEHIND a beam face / slab edge belongs to another panel. Drop = (beam top - slab top) x 100 compared in ONE datum (office-standards.md); beam level missing = the higher slab level (?); a drop > 150 = datum mix-up (flush + flag).
- **No marker of that side's own panel -> SOLID slab with the SYMBOL `H`** (drawn 25, flush), per side (`--d-label-left / --d-label-right`; `--d-label` both): never copy the other side's D, never a plan-wide note farther than 450 from the cut (reported, not applied: my 'D=80 from a note 994 away' was rejected). A user cloud 'casting completion' (השלמת יציקה) on the plan = a SOLID slab between the beams there: make that side solid, thickness symbol `H` if none given, level drop 0 (?).
- Ledge + solid pair: one LUH line = ledge on that side and the OTHER side (a `D=nn` marker without `+`, equal `o.k.` levels -> `--solid-drop 0`) is the solid casting completion; command for a west ledge, N view: `--kind ledge --sides left --solid right --solid-D 30 --solid-drop 0 --detail plan`. A solid slab low beside a tall stem (D + drop = H, soffit flush) is the user's gallery pattern (knowledge/hollow-core-geometries.md).
- **Free slab edge, no beam** ('ceiling edge'): D from a `D=nn` text within 450 of the cut, else the S_M markers, else the symbol `H` (`slab_edge.py --slab 25 --d-label H`, no thick extras). D >= 60 (? one example) = the thick detail. Orientation of the drawing: sections-drawing.md section 5.
- Wall above a beam / roller-shutter text near the cut: see sections-drawing.md; a sloped slab ALONG the cut line gets a label only.
- Cuts the planner cannot read: reported 'NOT DRAWN' with the reason - the user wants EVERY pair drawn, so read the cut by hand (`cut_faces` fallback), never leave one out. A solid block (`D=70` S_M at a beam junction, one `93_KORA` face and no beam B) has no taught recipe.

## 3. Decision table (step 3 of the star workflow)
tagged beam + CORES -> `ledge_beam --kind cast`; tagged + LENGTHWISE -> `ledge_beam --kind ledge` (`--slab a --topping b --sides both|left|right`); tagged without hollow evidence, or NO tag -> `general_beam.py`; free slab edge -> `slab_edge.py`; wall under the cut -> `general_beam` with B = wall thickness; nothing under the pair -> skip + report. Stirrup table: by the MARK (S / SS / `*` standard, ST transfer) - `transfer_check.py` is informational only. Roller-shutter text -> add the downstand (`slab_edge --shutter B H`; beams with the text: reported, not drawn).

## 4. Known planner gaps (roadmap items)
Untagged beam with a segment label `H=nnn`: read H from it; solid side + level step from `D=nn` without `+`; per-side thickness of untagged beams; panel membership of the sloped slab is not tested geometrically; ledge beam with a wall rising above and a roller shutter on a beam are not drawn.
