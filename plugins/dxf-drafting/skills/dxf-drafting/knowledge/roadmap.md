## Section-plan APPLICATION (user, 2026-10-23: 'once I finish teaching you, we will create an application for generating section plans')

1. Two slab types - SOLID and HOLLOW-CORE units (topping line / 'D=a+b' label vs 'D=nn'); the app must tell them apart and draw each by his rules (voids only for hollow-core where the cut crosses the panels).
