## The user's reading rules (taught 2026-10-13, each from one sentence of the user)
1. VIEW FROM BELOW: the plan is seen looking up at the slab soffit. Solid = visible from below (down-stand beams `93_KORA`, slab edge, cut walls/cores). Dashed/hidden = hidden by the slab = ABOVE it. `15_DASHED` = hidden lines (everything on it is above the slab); `82_KIR_OLE` (ACI 181 dashed) = walls/piers of the floor above, standing on this floor (matches the note 'beams rest on the wall of the floor above').

## What the builder must read (dimension priorities)
1. Position + width of every beam (both faces) and every wall/pier face (X and Y). 2. Clear span of each bay between beam/wall faces. 3. Every opening: size + position. 4. Slab edge incl. slanted edges. 5. Level changes (edge + tag).
Hidden (above-slab) items are dimensioned as hidden, never merged with visible faces.

## Habits
- Audit by FACES (`recipes/plan_audit.py`), coverage numbers in the report. Classify each polyline (beam pair, wall above, pier, opening, outline) before choosing a dimension type; never dimension a drawing artefact.
- Mistakes of 2026-10-13 to avoid: concluding 'leaders have no text' after searching only some text layers (search ALL TEXT near an entity, whatever its layer - layer `4` is also a text layer); reading the scale as m instead of cm; placing rooms from a quick render (tanks are NORTH); calling an area 'open hall/slab-less' from missing labels (rule 2); calling dashed items 'same level'.

- Opening marks (user 2026-10-13): the closed `6_HATCH` polygons (south opening, double-height void) only SHOW a double-height space around an opening; they carry no structure and are never dimensioned. Open 5-point `6_HATCH` symbols mark stairs/lifts/shafts.
- 15_DASHED thin strips (15 x ~1000) at beam faces = BEARING TEETH of plank (Luchad) beams (user 2026-10-13); they sit INSIDE the overall width drawn by the 93_KORA lines (80/65 beam = KORA faces 80 apart, teeth 15 + 50 + 15). Resolves the earlier open question about these strips. `0_LUHADIM_SHEN` (dashed) is the same family ('shen' = tooth).
- Stairs on `15_DASHED` (hidden = above the slab): equally spaced parallel step lines (tread 27) = a flight; the stair cell itself is the wall cell on 82_KIR_OLE + the 6_HATCH symbol.
- Stair marks (blue clouds): the user wants the stair as ONE block: overall length and width of its 15_DASHED drawing (east 335 x 233, west 125 x 54), nothing inside.
